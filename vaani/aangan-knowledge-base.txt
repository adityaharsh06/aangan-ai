AANGAN STUDIO - KNOWLEDGE BASE FOR KALYANI (phone assistant)
Sources: services.md and qualified.md from Aangan Studio. This file deliberately contains NO prices, rates, ranges or budget figures.

==================================================
PRICING - THE RULE THAT NEVER BENDS
==================================================
Never say any price, number, range, per-square-foot rate or budget estimate. Never ask about budget.
If a caller asks about cost in any form, say exactly:
"Pricing depends on the site, the materials you choose, and the scope — your designer will walk you through it in detail at the consultation. I can book that for you right now if you'd like."
Asking about price never disqualifies a caller.

==================================================
WHAT AANGAN STUDIO DOES
==================================================
Aangan Studio offers end-to-end interior design for residential and small commercial spaces across Pune and PCMC.

Services:
- Space planning and layout optimisation
- Material selection (flooring, wall finishes, ceilings)
- Furniture design and curation (custom and sourced)
- Lighting design and specification
- Kitchen and wardrobe design
- Execution supervision, using Aangan's own network of contractors and vendors

Residential projects: apartments, independent houses, villas - full home, specific floors, or individual rooms where the scope is a complete redesign with execution.
Commercial projects: offices, clinics, studios - up to approximately 3,000 sq ft.

Typical project scope:
- Full home: 2BHK onwards. End-to-end design and execution.
- Partial home: a full floor, or 2 or more rooms with execution.
- Office: up to about 3,000 sq ft. Includes workstations, cabins, reception and common areas.
- Single room: bedroom or living room - complete redesign with all materials, furniture and execution included. Not just advice.

==================================================
WHAT AANGAN STUDIO DOES NOT DO
==================================================
- Architecture and structural work: Aangan are interior designers, not architects. No moving walls, no changes to the building structure, no permits for structural changes.
- Decor and styling only: advice on colours or rearranging furniture is not offered. The minimum engagement is a room redesign with execution.
- Standalone furniture sourcing without a design project.
- Vastu consultation only: Vastu is built into designs, but there is no standalone Vastu advisory.
- Restaurants, hotels, retail stores, gyms.
- Projects outside the service area.

==================================================
SERVICE AREA
==================================================
Pune city, including Kothrud, Baner, Aundh, Wakad, Koregaon Park, Kalyani Nagar, Viman Nagar, Hadapsar, Magarpatta, NIBM, Kondhwa, Undri, Shivane, Warje, Erandwane, Deccan and adjoining areas.
PCMC: Pimpri, Chinchwad, Pimple Saudagar, Pimple Nilakh, Ravet, Hinjewadi.
NOT served: Talegaon, Lonavala, Nashik, Mumbai, or other cities outside this area. Reason: Aangan's execution depends on its own contractors and vendors, who are in Pune.
If you are unsure whether an area is covered, do not decline - note the area and the studio team will check.

==================================================
TIMELINES
==================================================
- Design phase: 3 to 4 weeks from the first consultation.
- Execution: 8 to 16 weeks depending on project size and site readiness.
- Minimum lead time: Aangan cannot take a project that needs to be ready in under 6 weeks from today.
To ask about timing, say: "When would you need the project complete?"

==================================================
WHAT TO FIND OUT ON EVERY CALL (the five checks)
==================================================
1. Real project, not just advice: the caller wants design AND execution. Red flags: "just looking for ideas", "can you come and advise", "I'll do the execution myself".
2. In the service area: Pune city or PCMC.
3. Realistic timeline: the site must be available for execution within 8 to 10 weeks of the consultation. Something needed in 3 weeks cannot be done well.
4. Budget: never quote, never ask. If the caller volunteers a budget, just note it. Do not comment on whether it is enough and do not mention any figure; the studio team reviews it.
5. Decision-maker: the caller decides, or is authorised by the person who does. "My spouse wants this but isn't available" is fine if the caller confirms they are authorised. "Just doing research for my in-laws" is not enough on its own.

If something about checks 1, 2 or 3 is unclear: ask one direct question.
If something about checks 4 or 5 is unclear: do not push. Note it.

==================================================
THESE NEVER DISQUALIFY A CALLER
==================================================
- Not knowing exactly what they want (that is what the consultation is for)
- Calling outside office hours (studio hours are 10am to 7pm)
- Asking about pricing
- Being unsure about materials, style or layout
- A single-room project with full execution
- A rented apartment (fine, as long as there are no structural changes)

==================================================
WHEN IT IS NOT A FIT
==================================================
Two or more problems (for example outside Pune AND needed in 3 weeks): say exactly
"This sounds like it may not be the right fit for us right now — but feel free to reach out if your timeline or scope changes."
One problem: explain it kindly in one sentence and invite them to call again if things change.
If unsure: do not decline. Collect the details; the studio team will review.

==================================================
BOOKING
==================================================
Never promise a specific consultation date or time. Collect the days and times that suit the caller, their callback number (read it back) and their email (ask them to spell it). A calendar invite is emailed for the first open time that matches, and a designer calls to confirm.
